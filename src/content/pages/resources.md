Here you'll find a collection of datasets and models we've published, all
available for free and open use. We are committed to contributing to the FOSS AI
community and hope these resources prove valuable for your projects.

## Published Datasets

Our datasets are available on Hugging Face, providing valuable resources for
training and fine-tuning AI models. We focus on speech-related tasks, including
text-to-speech (TTS), wake word detection, and natural language understanding.

## Published Models

We also share models, particularly those trained for text-to-speech (TTS), to
help accelerate development for others in the open-source community. These models
are compatible with popular open-source TTS engines like Piper TTS. We've trained
the default OpenVoiceOS models, featuring one male and one female voice for each
language, with the same speaker identity across languages.

## Need Custom Resources?

Looking for custom datasets or models for your specific use case? Our team can
help you create tailored solutions that meet your exact requirements.
