In a world of data-hungry voice assistants, there is a better way. TigreGótico
builds voice technology on privacy, open source, and data sovereignty. As
creators of the HiveMind stack and core contributors to OpenVoiceOS, we help you ship secure,
GDPR-compliant voice solutions that run on your own hardware — no data sent to
anyone else's cloud.

## What We've Built

- **91 open-source projects** spanning voice AI, NLP, data extraction, and games — all freely available
- **124+ datasets on Hugging Face** — phonetics, TTS training data, intent recognition, media metadata, and more
- **2 voice models** (Miro & Dii) in 20+ languages, powering OpenVoiceOS and Home Assistant
- **Core contributor** to OpenVoiceOS — our founder serves on the foundation's board
- **Battle-tested stack** behind everything from voice assistants to privacy-first web scraping
