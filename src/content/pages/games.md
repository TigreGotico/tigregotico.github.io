We build games you play by reading and speaking — not by staring at a screen.
From classic interactive fiction to dynamic, LLM-powered generative worlds, every
title runs in your terminal or entirely by voice. They are designed audio-first,
so they are playable hands-free and by blind and low-vision players, anywhere.

## TNaLaGmes *(beta)*

**T(oolbox) Na(tural) La(nguage) G(a)mes** is our framework for building and running natural-language voice games. It provides the engine behind dozens of titles — from classic simulations like Oregon Trail and Lunar Lander to original adventures — handling game state, voice I/O, and NLP so authors can focus on the story and the rules.

[Browse TNaLaGmes games on GitHub →](https://github.com/VoiceGamez/TNaLaGmes)

## StoryForge *(beta)*

**StoryForge** is our engine for story-driven interactive fiction — richer, branching narratives where choices matter and the world reacts to the player. The flagship title is *Tower of Silence*, a full voice adventure built with blind and low-vision players as the primary audience.

[Browse StoryForge games on GitHub →](https://github.com/VoiceGamez/story-forge)

## pyFrotz

**[pyFrotz](https://github.com/VoiceGamez/pyFrotz)** is our Python wrapper around the Frotz Z-machine interpreter, enabling voice playback of classic Z5 story files. Beyond running the originals, we use it to expand and translate classic Infocom and community-made games — bringing them to new languages and voice-first audiences.
