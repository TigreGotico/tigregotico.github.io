We build games you play by reading and speaking — not by staring at a screen.
From classic interactive fiction to dynamic, LLM-powered generative worlds, every
title runs in your terminal or entirely by voice. They are designed audio-first,
so they are playable hands-free and by blind and low-vision players, anywhere.
