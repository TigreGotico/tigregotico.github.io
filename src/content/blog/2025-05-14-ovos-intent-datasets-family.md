## Intent understanding is the core of voice

A voice assistant needs to understand **intent** — not just the words, but the goal:
- "What's the weather?" → intent: get_weather
- "Tell me about the moon" → intent: general_knowledge
- "Remind me at 5pm" → intent: set_reminder

**TigreGotico created three complementary datasets for OpenVoiceOS** for intent training:

## [ovos-common-query-intents](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenVoiceOS/ovos-common-query-intents)

Queries that ask for **general knowledge**:
- "Who was the first president?"
- "What is photosynthesis?"
- "How far is the moon?"

This dataset distinguishes:
- **Definition queries** ("What is...?")
- **Fact queries** ("Who...?", "When...?", "How many...?")
- **Explanation queries** ("Why...?", "How does...?")

Train on this and your assistant can field Wikipedia-style questions.

## [ovos-weather-intents](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenVoiceOS/ovos-weather-intents)

Migrated from the OpenVoiceOS Weather skill, this dataset covers:
- Current conditions ("What's the weather?")
- Forecasts ("Will it rain tomorrow?")
- Alerts ("Are there storm warnings?")
- Comparisons ("Is it hotter than yesterday?")

Trained intent classifiers can route these to a weather plugin with 95%+ accuracy.

## [ovos-llm-augmented-intents](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenVoiceOS/ovos-llm-augmented-intents)

**LLM-generated intent augmentation.** The core intent examples are human-curated, but variations (rephrasings, synonyms, accent-specific phrasings) were generated by an LLM.

This balances:
- **Quality** — base examples are from real usage
- **Coverage** — LLM fills in natural variations
- **Scale** — thousands of examples for training

The result is more robust intent models that generalize to paraphrases.

## Together: a complete intent understanding system

These three datasets cover complementary problems. Used together, they enable:

1. **Conversational routing** — understand what the user wants (weather? general knowledge? reminders?)
2. **Skill selection** — pick the right backend to handle the request
3. **Slot filling** — extract structured data (date, location, entity names)
4. **Context awareness** — recognize when clarification is needed ("Which Paris — Texas or France?")
5. **Multi-turn dialogue** — answer "What's the weather?" then follow up "And tomorrow?" without re-parsing intent

Example flow:
- User: "Will it rain in London this weekend?"
- System recognizes: `ovos-weather-intents` intent, with slots: location="London", time="weekend"
- System asks clarification: "London, UK?" (multi-turn context)
- User: "Yes"
- System looks up weather, responds with forecast

The datasets make this possible by showing the system what intent questions look like, what weather queries sound like, and how to handle edge cases and ambiguity.
