## Preservation through publication

Linguistic corpora are fragile. They live in university servers, archived on CDs, documented in papers from 1995. When the lab closes, when funding ends, when the researcher retires — the corpus vanishes.

We archived and mirrored three valuable Portuguese linguistic corpora on HuggingFace for preservation and accessibility:

## [Synapse](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TigreGotico/synapse)

**Mirrored dataset.** A **syntactic annotation corpus** from the University of Granada (original source: https://www.ugr.es). We archived it to ensure permanence and accessibility. Portuguese sentences with full syntactic trees, POS tags, and dependency relations.

Use this for:
- Training syntactic parsers
- Studying Portuguese sentence structure
- Grammatical research
- NLP pipeline development

## [Ludvic](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TigreGotico/ludvic)

**Mirrored dataset.** **Aligned speech and text corpus** from CLUL (original source: https://cl.up.pt). Audio recordings with time-aligned transcriptions and linguistic annotations. We preserved it on HuggingFace.

Use this for:
- Training speech recognition models
- Studying prosody and timing
- Annotated speech research
- Acoustic phonetics

## [mirandese-ipa-dict-synthetic](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TigreGotico/mirandese-ipa-dict-synthetic)

**Original dataset.** A Mirandese (endangered Iberian language, ~14K speakers) **IPA dictionary with synthetic audio examples generated by TigreGotico**. Each word:

```json
{
  "word": "...",
  "ipa": "...",
  "audio": "...synthetic speech...",
  "language": "mwl"
}
```

Use this for:
- Documenting Mirandese phonology
- Training G2P models for Mirandese
- Speech synthesis for language preservation
- Linguistic research on endangered languages

## Why this matters

These corpora represent decades of linguistic work. By archiving them openly:

1. **Preservation** — the data survives beyond the original project
2. **Access** — researchers anywhere can use them without institutional login
3. **Curation** — we verify completeness, fix corruption, document provenance
4. **Citation** — HuggingFace gives them permanent DOIs

Portuguese linguistic infrastructure is now safer.
