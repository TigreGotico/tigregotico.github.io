## The short version

This website collects **no personal data**. No cookies, no analytics, no
tracking pixels, no web forms, no third-party embeds. We build privacy-first
voice technology, and our own website is held to the same standard.

## What this site does

- **Static pages only.** The site is plain HTML/CSS served from GitHub Pages.
  There is no server-side code of ours and no database.
- **No cookies.** Your theme and colour-palette choices are stored in your
  browser's `localStorage` — they never leave your device and contain no
  identifying information.
- **Self-hosted fonts and assets.** Every resource on regular pages is served
  from this site's own origin; no requests go to Google, CDNs, or ad networks.
- **No analytics.** We don't count you, fingerprint you, or watch what you read.

## Hosting

The site is served by **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host,
GitHub may log standard technical data (IP address, user agent) to operate and
secure the service; see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for details. We have no access to, and keep no copy of, those logs.

## The voices demo

The interactive [text-to-speech demo](/demo) runs **entirely in your browser**.
Nothing you type is sent to us. When you pick a voice, your browser downloads
the open model files from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co), which is
subject to the [Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).
Synthesis itself happens on your device.

## Email

If you [contact us](/contact) by email, we use what you send us solely to
answer you. We don't add you to mailing lists and we don't share your address
with anyone.
