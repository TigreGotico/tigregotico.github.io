Everything we ship is real, honest, and built on open source. Our products extend
the OpenVoiceOS stack and support the community that maintains it.

## OpenVoiceOS Plugins & Integrations

Plugins that extend OVOS — solvers, embeddings, STT and TTS adapters, and pipeline
components — built and maintained by the core team. They follow a "freemium"
model: you pay to download, but the code stays BSD-licensed, so you keep full
freedom to use, study, and modify it. The released work is listed on our Projects
page.

## Custom Offline TTS Voice Training

A unique, fully-offline voice for your application. Bring a dataset or a reference
voice and we train a high-quality TTS model that runs locally and drops into
OpenVoiceOS or Home Assistant. Scoped as an engineering engagement on our Services
page.

## Support & Engineering

Hands-on integration, troubleshooting, and feature work from the people who
maintain the stack. Engagement options — onboarding, retainer, and hourly — are
described on our Services page.

## Official OVOS Merchandise

Every sticker and magnet you buy is a direct investment in the open-source
community. Proceeds support the independent OpenVoiceOS non-profit foundation and
the ongoing maintenance of the stack.
